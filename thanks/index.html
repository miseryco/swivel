Install Swivel

Download for Mac

Open the disk image, drag Swivel to Applications, then eject the disk image. Open Swivel from Applications and follow the camera setup guide.

Activate this Mac

  1. In your Polar order email, choose Access purchase and copy your license key from the customer portal.
  2. In Swivel, choose Settings → License → Enter License Key… and paste your key.
