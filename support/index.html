Swivel support

Most setup issues come from camera permission, a disconnected camera, or the one-time camera extension approval. Start with these steps.

Set up Swivel

  1. Open Swivel and select Output in the sidebar.
  2. Choose Activate Camera.
  3. Allow Swivel under System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions → Camera Extensions. An administrator may need to approve it.
  4. Return to Swivel and choose Restart Swivel if requested.
  5. Add a physical camera, grant camera permission, and enable Swivel.
  6. Select Swivel Virtual Camera in your compatible video app.

Camera permission is denied

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera, enable Swivel, then quit and reopen it. On a managed Mac, ask the administrator whether the setting is restricted.

A camera is disconnected

Reconnect the device, unlock a Continuity Camera iPhone, or choose Refresh in the Add Cameras window. If another app is using the camera exclusively, close that app and try again.

The virtual camera is missing

Confirm that the camera extension is approved, restart Swivel when prompted, and relaunch your video app so it refreshes its camera list. Restarting your Mac can clear a stale camera list after an extension update.

Remove Swivel

Quit Swivel and move it from Applications to the Trash in Finder. macOS manages removal of its camera extension. Managed or scripted installations may require the administrator’s normal system-extension removal policy.

Contact support

Email swivel@misery.co. Include your macOS version, Mac model, camera models, and the exact message shown by Swivel.
