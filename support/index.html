Swivel support

Swivel runs on macOS 14 or later. For automatic switching, add two or more cameras. You can use built-in webcams, USB cameras, or an iPhone with Continuity Camera.

Install the direct download

  1. Download Swivel for macOS and open the disk image.
  2. Drag Swivel onto the Applications shortcut, then eject the disk image.
  3. Open Swivel from Applications so macOS can activate its virtual camera.

Set up your cameras

  1. Open Swivel, choose Get Started, then Activate Camera.
  2. Choose Open Camera Extension Settings when prompted. On macOS 15 or later, go to General → Login Items & Extensions, open Camera Extensions and turn on Swivel. On macOS 14, choose Allow in Privacy & Security. You may need administrator approval.
  3. Follow any restart prompt. In Swivel, choose Allow Camera Access, select your cameras and finish setup.
  4. In your video app’s camera menu, select Swivel Virtual Camera.

Use a direct license

You can try the direct download for as long as you like. During the trial, calls and recordings include a Swivel watermark. Activating a direct license clears it, even during a call.

Choose Access purchase in your Polar order email and copy your license key from the customer portal. In Swivel, open Settings → License → Enter License Key… and paste your key. An activated copy works offline.

One key covers up to three Macs. To move it to another Mac, choose Deactivate This Mac… in Swivel Settings or remove the old Mac from the portal. Mac App Store purchases use Apple’s licensing and don’t need a key.

Camera permission is denied

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera, enable Swivel, then quit and reopen it. On a managed Mac, ask the administrator whether the setting is restricted.

A camera is disconnected

Reconnect the device, unlock a Continuity Camera iPhone, or choose Refresh in the Add Cameras window. If another app is using the camera exclusively, close that app and try again.

The virtual camera is missing

Open Output in Swivel’s sidebar to check the extension. Choose Activate Camera if it needs activation and follow the prompts. Relaunch your video app to refresh its camera list. If the camera is still missing after an extension update, restart your Mac.

Update Swivel

For the direct download, choose Check for Updates… in Swivel’s Settings or its menu-bar icon’s right-click menu. For an App Store copy, open the Mac App Store’s Updates page. If Swivel shows Update Swivel Camera after an update, choose it and follow the prompts to resume output.

Switch between versions

Quit Swivel before replacing it in Applications. The two versions share your settings and use one virtual camera. Each uses the purchase from its own store. Keep your Polar key if you return to the direct download, and deactivate this Mac first if you want to use its slot elsewhere.

Remove Swivel

Quit Swivel and move it to the Trash. If Swivel Virtual Camera still appears in video apps, open System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions, view Extensions By Category, click the info button beside Camera Extensions, and choose Delete Extension from the three-dot menu beside Swivel Virtual Camera.

Contact support

Email swivel@misery.co. Include your macOS version, Mac model, camera models, and the exact message shown by Swivel.
