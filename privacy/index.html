Privacy policy

Swivel processes camera feeds and attention estimates on your Mac. How Swivel handles purchases, licensing, and updates depends on where you got the app.

Camera data

Both versions process camera frames, face landmarks, and attention scores locally. Swivel has no advertising, analytics, or tracking.

On-device processing

Swivel uses Apple camera and Vision technologies on your Mac to estimate which configured camera has your attention. Video is transformed locally and supplied to the Swivel Virtual Camera extension. Swivel does not store the frames or analysis results.

Local preferences

Swivel stores camera identifiers and names, your Home camera, switching sensitivity, output settings, enabled state, and camera-extension version in its local shared app container. These preferences stay on your Mac. You can remove them by deleting Swivel and its local data.

Apple and the Mac App Store

The Mac App Store version of Swivel collects no personal data and makes no network requests. Apple handles its purchases and updates. Apple may process purchase, download, diagnostic, or storefront information under its own terms and privacy policy. Swivel does not receive your payment details.

Direct purchases and licenses

Polar is the merchant of record for purchases from this website. It processes your payment and provides a license key in its customer portal. When you activate Swivel, the app sends Polar your key, Swivel’s organization identifier, and the name of your Mac. The name can contain your own name and helps you recognize the Mac in the portal. Swivel keeps the key and activation identifier in your Mac’s Keychain. It sends the key and both identifiers to Polar to check the license or deactivate this Mac.

Direct-download updates

The direct-download version uses Sparkle to check misery.co for new versions and download updates. You can change automatic update checks in Swivel’s Settings.

Changes to this policy

If Swivel’s data practices change, this policy and its App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the changed version is released.

Contact

For questions about this policy or Swivel’s data practices, email swivel@misery.co.
