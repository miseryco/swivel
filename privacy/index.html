Privacy policy

Swivel does its work on your Mac. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or use personal data for tracking.

Data Swivel does not collect

Swivel has no analytics, advertising, tracking, account system, or third-party SDKs. Camera frames, face landmarks, and attention scores are not sent to Misery & Company Ltd. or any other service.

On-device processing

Swivel uses Apple camera and Vision technologies on your Mac to estimate which configured camera has your attention. Video is transformed locally and supplied to the Swivel Virtual Camera extension. Swivel does not store the frames or analysis results.

Preferences stored on your Mac

Swivel stores camera identifiers and names, your Home camera, switching sensitivity, output settings, enabled state, and camera-extension version in its local shared app container. These preferences stay on your Mac. You can remove them by deleting Swivel and its local data.

Apple and the Mac App Store

Apple may process purchase, download, diagnostic, or storefront information under its own terms and privacy policy. Swivel does not receive your payment details.

Changes to this policy

If Swivel’s data practices change, this policy and its App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the changed version is released.

Contact

For questions about this policy or Swivel’s data practices, email swivel@misery.co.
